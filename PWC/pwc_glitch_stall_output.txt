6. SIGN CHECK (the locked framework says the medium does not move)
==============================================================================
  The ambient medium is stationary (locked ruling). A shell re-catching
  stationary medium absorbs its momentum, which in the rotating frame
  points AGAINST the spin: the catch is a spin-DOWN of magnitude m*v*R,
  not a spin-up.  For dn/nu > 0 the swept medium must already carry
  specific angular momentum > v*R at the shell - no locked mechanism
  gives the ambient any rotation.  Magnitude fails (sec. 3); sign also fails.
