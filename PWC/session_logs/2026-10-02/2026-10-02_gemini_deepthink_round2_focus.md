## Review (Claude, 2026-10-02)
- **T3.** (1 + 2S)/3 is `OPEN_WORK.md` line 131 verbatim (rigidity w = (1 + 2S)/3). It is also an identity: for a gas of waves P_parallel = eps*<cos^2 theta> and <cos^2 theta> = (1 + 2S)/3 (checked: isotropic S = 0 gives 0.3332). So the formula is correct but it is not new and not a derivation of the transition: the function S(rho, T) between S = 0 and S = 1 (Onsager compression vs Maier-Saupe heat) is not supplied. Only the two end points are used.
- **T11.** The two halves are asserted equal, as in OPEN_WORK line 112 (each term "NEEDS DERIVATION"). The text contradicts itself: "severe structural birefringence" and "no polarization splitting". The mapping onto eps and mu (n = sqrt(eps*mu) with each shifted by 2GM/(r c0^2), n = 1 + 2GM/(r c0^2)) is the same total as the push/pull of row 14 but is a different account (mu/eps instead of pull/push) and gives no reason for the shifts to be equal.
- **T1.** Arithmetic is right (Gamma_untie*c0 = 7.165e-10; /2pi = 1.140e-10; the 2.39e-18 s^-1 is H0 = 73.7 km/s/Mpc). But a0 = c*H0/(2 pi) is the known Milgrom coincidence, and the 2 pi is chosen to land near MOND's 1.2e-10 (6% off). The repo's own a0 is 6.68e-11 (measured inside its formula g_obs = g_bar(1 + sqrt(a0/g_bar))), reproduced by the T1 derivation a_hold = (c0/sqrt3)*sqrt(G*rho0/4) = 6.61e-11 (1%) with no H0 and no 2 pi. So there is no "gap" in the repo's own frame. 1.14e-10 is 1.71x the repo's a0; in the deep-MOND regime g_obs scales as sqrt(a0), an offset of 0.116 dex against the fit's 0.1327 dex scatter (estimate; not run on SPARC). The T1 x T7 product invariant (0.02%) also relies on 6.61e-11. For 6.68e-11 the required factor is 10.73 = 3.41 pi, not 2 pi.
- **T6.** Plausible and not GR: solutions of a d'Alembert-type field equation with speed c0 contract by sqrt(1 - v^2/c0^2) when set in motion (Lorentz invariance of that equation). The text states the conclusion and the "unspooling under dynamic load" story; the steps (a boost of a solution of the wave equation) are not shown. Acceptable only if the knot is a solution of such an equation with c0 as the limit; then it is a short derivation to write out.
