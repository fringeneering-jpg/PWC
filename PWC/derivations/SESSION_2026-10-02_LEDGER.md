## 1. His rulings (in force)
- **Black hole = core + ρ_max shell.** "Shell" = the ρ_max layer only (starts where Max-P starts); outside is the heavy medium, the "starting attractor" (name not final), falling off by the medium's own pull on itself; same for every hole, so not computed. - **Core:** neutron density, "just trying to not beta decay", stacks like the medium (adds layers at the density ceiling, never density); no beta-decay wall. The interior of a big core **keeps pulling but has no effect outside**, so it is removed from the core's pulling capacity; **no cap** on surface pull (it can pull over P_max and the shell just stacks thicker). Heavier cores are denser (self-compression): "neutrons wouldn't be easy to compress, but neither is water, bet it will for 100 million suns, more than for 19".
- **The densities and P_max came from the single GW150914 snapshot** ("we derived the density of the neutron cores and the Pmax and all that from the famous 150914 data"), then assumed the same for every core — the source of the mass-dependent error. He also holds (later) that the yield is a pure PWC ceiling from ρ_max; derivation not in git; origin unresolved (DERIVATIONS.md Gaps row).
- **Merger:** cores push through each other's ρ_max layers (the build-up; not instant); incompressible volumes add; the joined core has volumetrically less max pull / storage capacity and drops mass. **Energy and mass are the same thing; heat is a separate thing and is not involved in the merger.** The ~3 M☉ is added medium that stays (no return to rest); "ringdown" is forbidden vocabulary. "Volumes are the issue, masses match perfectly ... overestimating the neutrons' pulling power a tiny bit, perfect naturally at the ~50 km range." The drop is limited by the smaller of motion energy and storage ("the volumetrically shrinking capability to store medium with a volumetrically larger core").
- **The medium does not move.** Matter is a sieve; a sieve swung fast enough chokes; every density has its own speed limit. 954 km/s is the medium's own limit at Max-P (RBH-1's outer shell chokes and forms a new Max-P front; an uncovered core would have beta-decayed). Light and Max-P are the exact same thing at different densities. Supernova explosions and the merger are further Max-P bow waves. The core alone would choke at ≈ 72 km/s but with minimal drag (small frontal area) unless the Max-P layer diverts the drag.
- **Lensing:** pull + push (two forces, different causes, equal by equilibrium); photons are the LDL squeezed by the HDL; untying knots grow the path; the medium does not move, matter does; birefringence helps redshift. a₀: constant outward push of the resting medium against the inward pull of a dense core (T1, 6.61×10⁻¹¹ vs 6.68×10⁻¹¹).
- **Cavitation:** vapour back to liquid expels heat and needs the bow wave: that is water's limit; Chernobyl steam voids are the cavitation pockets; the implosion before the explosion was the neutron-shielding hydrogen cavitating. Evaporation is continuous; boiling is a chosen point; the water sound-speed peak is a blip.
