## Mechanics
Cavitation is vapour returning to liquid; that expels heat and needs the bow wave (the medium forced around a body faster than it can close behind); that is water's speed limit. At Chernobyl the water running out into steam makes the cavitation pockets, and the implosion before the explosion was the neutron-shielding hydrogen cavitating. Light at c₀ and the ρ_max shell at 954 km/s are the same choke at two densities. The medium does not move.
