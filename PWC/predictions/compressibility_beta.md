# Prediction frozen before running — medium compressibility β from mergers (Jaden, 2026-09-25)

Model: held medium per m² of core surface grows with the core's surface pull, Σ ∝ g_c^β (g_c ∝ C^(1/3)), i.e.
M = C + K·C^p with p = 2/3 + β/3 (β = 0 is the current k-rule; β = 1 is scale-free like GR).
K re-anchored so GW150914 (GWTC masses 35.6 + 30.6 → 63.1) stays exact for every β. Cores conserved, C_f = C1 + C2.
**β fitted on GWTC-4.0 only** (84 BBHs, minimize RMS deviation); then frozen and applied blind to the 104 GWTC-5.0 BBHs.
Predictions on GWTC-5.0: (1) std < 1.40% (current k-rule); (2) |corr(C_f, deviation)| < 0.3; (3) mean within ±1%.
Caveat: catalogue final masses come from GR remnant formulas.

## Outcome — the three numbers PASSED, but the fit abandoned the core picture
Fitted on GWTC-4.0: β = 0.791 (p = 0.930), K = 9,740 — at the edge of the feasible range (GWTC-4.0 rms 1.71% at β=0 → 0.98%).
GWTC-5.0 blind: mean −0.30% (±1% ✓), std 0.90% (<1.40% ✓), corr +0.161 (|·|<0.3 ✓), 101/104 within 2%; k-rule was −0.86% / 1.40% / +0.699. Small-merger bias −2.57% → −0.66%.
**Mechanism check (after the run):** with K = 9,740 the "core" is ~0.007% of every black hole (0.004 M☉ of 63) — the model has become M_f ≈ (m₁^(1/p) + m₂^(1/p))^p, a scale-free mass combination with no core. The fit improved by going to the limit where GR's remnant formula (itself scale-free) is copied, not by adding compressibility to a core-plus-medium black hole. So this does NOT measure the medium's compressibility; it shows the GR-derived catalogue values prefer scale-free release. A compressibility test needs β derived (or bounded) with the core kept physical (core ≈ 80% of the mass, as in §0).
