## 6. Heat-spacer scan: can it fail?

Sign predicted from the input alone (-d2f/dd dT at d0, given f''>0) matches the
scan outcome in 540/540 rows, with no minimization needed to state it.
Implicit-function theorem: dd0/dT = -(d2f/dd dT) / f''(d0). Families A-D were written
with a negative mixed derivative and E with a positive one, so once f'' > 0 the sign
is fixed. The scan verifies the optimiser and the bookkeeping; it cannot fail on the
physics, and 432/432 + 108/108 is not evidence about PWC. A test that can fail needs a
thermal term whose d-dependence is derived from the medium, not chosen to point outward.

## 7. The 2026-09-30 equilibrium closure (t2_equilibrium.py)

Reproduction: prefactor, B and the +-1% responses in the supplied cell output are
reproduced to every printed digit (prefactor 2.1745131423320837e-27, B 273244563.18055946).
The printed responses match dρ/dt = B*rho*(1 - rho/rho*) exactly, a logistic form whose
fixed point is stable for ANY rho* and B > 0. 'PASS: pushes toward the fixed point' is
therefore true by construction and carries no information about rho*.
'Required eta*<v>/c0 = 0.2488' is prefactor / target: defined from the target, not a test.

### 7a. What the closure gives

rho_0 = 8*a0*rho_max*R_p / (3*eta*c0*<v>) = 8.6981e-27 kg/m^3 at eta = 1/4, <v> = c0
  = 0.9948 x the historical value (0.95*rho_crit(70)). Uses no measured H0 or G.
That agreement (0.5%) is the interesting fact. Points 7b-7f are why it is not yet a derivation.

### 7b. rho_0 cancels from the balance until G is replaced

| rho_0 tried | create/decay (measured G) |
|---:|---:|
| 1.00e-27 | 0.8610 |
| 8.74e-27 | 0.8610 |
| 1.00e-24 | 0.8610 |

create/decay does not depend on rho_0: with measured G the balance fixes nothing about
rho_0, and instead requires eta*<v>/c0 = a0/(pi*G*rho_max*R_p) = 0.2904 (not 0.25).
rho_0 is fixed only by substituting G = 3*Gamma^2/(8*pi*rho_0): the Friedmann relation with
rho_0 as the critical density and H := Gamma_untie. That is rho_crit re-entering through
an added axiom (H0 = Gamma_untie), not a derivation that is free of it.

### 7c. The substituted G disagrees with the measured G

G_emergent(rho_0 = 8.698e-27) = 7.7519e-11  vs measured 6.6743e-11  (ratio 1.161).
G is measured to ~2e-5. The closure fixes rho_0 by assuming an emergent G that is 16% off.

### 7d. <v> = c0 carries the result

  <v> = c0                                     -> rho_0 = 8.698e-27 kg/m^3
  <v> = 370 km/s (CMB dipole; OPEN_WORK.md)    -> rho_0 = 7.048e-24 kg/m^3
The repo elsewhere puts bodies at ~370 km/s through the medium; the closure needs c0.
eta = 1/4 is also justified two ways: photon-gas flux u*c/4 in T1, swept plane over
sphere (pi r^2 / 4 pi r^2) here. Same number, two different derivations. For T1 it was chosen
after 1/pi missed; whether it was fixed before the target for T2 is not documented.

### 7e. a0 is an input, and T1 then 'predicts' a0

CLOSURE_SHEET lists rho_0's allowed inputs as expansion/propagation/cosmology, NOT galaxy
rotation, and lists a0 as the sealed target. The closure takes a0 (a SPARC fit) as input.
a_hold(rho_0 from closure) = 6.5941e-11  (a0 = 6.680e-11, ratio 0.987).
With Omega_eff = eta*<v>/c0 = 1/4, algebraically a_hold^2 = (8/9)*G*a0*rho_max*R_p, so the T1 match is
the statement
a0 ~ (8/9)*G*rho_max*R_p = 6.5093e-11 (2.6% from the fit). rho_0 drops out.
H0_wall = rho_0*B = 8*a0/(3*eta*<v>) -> 73.34 km/s/Mpc; c*H0/a0 = 10.667 predicted vs 10.623 (SH0ES), 9.803 (Planck).
So T1, T7 and the product all reduce to functions of one fitted number (a0 = 6.68e-11,
which is form-dependent: the RAR fit gives ~1.2e-10, which this closure would turn into
H0 = 132 km/s/Mpc).

### 7f. Verdict on this section

Real: an explicit equation now exists, reproduces the cell, and lands within 0.5% of the
historical rho_0. Not yet real: independence. It needs (i) the Friedmann axiom H := Gamma,
(ii) <v> = c0, (iii) eta = 1/4, (iv) a0 as input, and it implies G 16% off. Status: a
candidate closure with a striking coincidence, not a derivation that removes the H0 input.
