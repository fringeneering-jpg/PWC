# Prediction frozen before running — 43 GeV line follows hot gas, not dark matter (Jaden, 2026-09-25)
Data: Fan et al. arXiv:2407.11737v3 — line detected toward Virgo, Fornax, Ophiuchus; upper limits only for the other 10 (M49, A3526, A1060, Coma, NGC 4636, AWM7, A1367, NGC 5813, A2877, S636). Positions, z, J-factors from their Table I.
Hot gas: MCXC (Piffaretti+2011, J/A+A/534/A109) L500 (0.1–2.4 keV) matched by position (nearest within 1°); X-ray flux proxy F = L500 / (4π d²), d = cz/H0 (H0 = 70).
Test: rank the 13 by X-ray flux. PWC (matter → energy in the hot medium): the 3 detected clusters rank above the 10 non-detections (one-sided Mann–Whitney p < 0.05). Also reported: the same test with the dark-matter J-factor ranking.
Limit: only 3 detections; binary detected / not detected.

## Outcome — not met
X-ray flux ranks of the 3 detections: Virgo 1, Ophiuchus 4, Fornax 8 (of 11 matched; M49 and S636 not in MCXC). One-sided Mann–Whitney p = 0.19. Centaurus (A3526, rank 2) and Coma (rank 3) are X-ray bright with no line; Fornax is X-ray faint with a line.
J-factor ranking: the 3 detections are the top 3, p = 0.006 — but partly circular: the paper selected and stacked its 'first three' by J-factor and reports best fits only for them.
